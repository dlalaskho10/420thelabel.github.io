About 420 THE Label

420 THE Label is an independent music label based in Umkomaas, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa.

Our mission is to develop fresh artists, create original sounds and connect South African music with listeners everywhere.

From Gqom and Amapiano to Afrobeat, Hip-Hop, Trap and Drill — this is where different sounds come together.
